Why there is no public talent directory

We protect the privacy of the professionals in our network. Their details are shared with a company only when our team presents them for a specific job request, and contact details stay with VerifyTalent Global.

How to start

  1. Register your company and tell us your recruitment needs.
  2. We review your company and send a client agreement.
  3. Once you are active, post a job request with the skills, experience, and terms you need.
  4. We score verified talent, review the results, and present candidates. Scores are internal guides, not a guarantee of suitability or hiring.
