About VerifyTalent Global

Find. Verify. Match.

VerifyTalent Global connects companies with qualified remote professionals by finding, screening, verifying, onboarding, and matching talent with suitable job opportunities.

What we do

Professionals apply to join our talent network for free. Our team reviews each application, and approved professionals complete a verification and onboarding step. Companies register separately, are reviewed, and sign a client agreement before they can request talent.

When a company posts a job request, our team scores verified talent against it, reviews the results, and presents suitable candidates. Companies and talent then arrange interviews through us.

What we do not do

We do not promise jobs, interviews, offers, or placements to professionals, and we do not promise hires to companies. Matching scores are internal guides, and every match is reviewed by a person.
