How it works

For professionals

  1. Register for free. Create an account and tell us about your profession, skills, and experience.
  2. Screening. Our team reviews your application. We may ask you to complete an assessment.
  3. Approval. If you pass the initial review, you can move to onboarding. Approval is not a job offer.
  4. Verification and onboarding. You pay the one-time Talent Verification & Onboarding Fee of $60 directly to our official administrator. We confirm the payment manually. There is no online payment on this site.
  5. Talent ID. Once payment is confirmed, you receive a VerifyTalent Global Talent ID and a digital ID card with a verification link.
  6. Matching. Verified talent is considered when relevant client roles open. You choose whether you are interested in each opportunity.

What the $60 covers: application processing, professional screening/review, verification, talent profile creation, qualification/document review where applicable, your official VerifyTalent Global ID card, verified talent network onboarding, eligibility for relevant job matching, and onboarding support.

Payment does not guarantee employment, an interview, a job offer, or placement.

For companies

  1. Register your company. Share your business details and recruitment needs.
  2. Company review and approval. We review every company before anything is activated.
  3. Client agreement. We send an agreement for you to read and accept.
  4. Activation. After approval and acceptance, your account is activated and you receive an official Client ID.
  5. Job requests. Post what you need. Our team scores verified talent, reviews the results, and presents suitable candidates.
  6. Interview and hire. Request interviews with presented candidates. We arrange them with the talent.

What verification means

Verification at VerifyTalent Global means we have reviewed a professional's application and completed our onboarding checks. It is not a government identification, and it is not a guarantee of any person's skills, conduct, or suitability for a particular job.
